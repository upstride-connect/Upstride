Questions

Before you book

Who is this for?

Students, recent graduates and working professionals who want help with interviews, a career switch, a promotion or their resume.

Are sessions online?

Yes. All one-to-one sessions run on video.

How are guides verified?

Our team checks each applicant's LinkedIn profile, current role and experience of interviewing or leading people in their field.

How do I pay?

Online payment is being added. Until then, your guide shares payment details with you directly once they accept your session.

How is this different from AI mock interview apps?

AI apps are useful for unlimited practice. Here, an experienced person questions you live and gives advice based on real hiring decisions.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. We help you prepare and show you where you stand. The offer depends on you and the employer.
