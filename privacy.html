Privacy notice

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This notice explains what personal data NextRung collects, why, who can see it and the choices you have. It applies to this website and the dashboards for learners, guides and the NextRung team.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only in encrypted form by our sign-in provider) and whether you are a learner or a guide.
  • Learner profile: career stage, field and main goal, and an optional phone number.
  • Guide profile: current role, field, years of experience, a short bio, LinkedIn link, usual availability, services and prices, an optional phone number and an optional UPI ID for payments.
  • Profile photo, if you upload one.
  • Sessions: bookings, time slots, notes you add to a booking, meeting links, scorecards written by guides, and ratings and comments written by learners.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show verified guides to learners and let learners book, reschedule and cancel sessions.
  • To verify guides before they appear in the directory.
  • To let guides prepare for a session and give written feedback afterwards.
  • To keep the service safe and to look into problems you report.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Who can see what

  • Everyone: a verified guide's name, photo, role, field, experience, bio, LinkedIn link, services, prices, open times, ratings and review comments.
  • A guide you book: your name, photo, career stage, field, goal and the note you add to the booking.
  • A learner whose session you accepted: your UPI ID, so they can pay you. It is not shown to anyone else.
  • Only you and the NextRung team: your email address and phone number.

Services we rely on

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Video calls run on the meeting service in the session link (Jitsi Meet, Google Meet or Zoom), which has its own privacy terms. The site keeps your sign-in session in your browser's local storage; it does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, photo and contact details. We may keep booking records for a limited time where we need them to settle a dispute or meet a legal duty.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and correct your details at any time in your dashboard under Profile.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to delete your account, or withdraw your consent by writing to .
  • If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Who can use NextRung

NextRung is for people aged 18 or over.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we will also tell you in your dashboard.
